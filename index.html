Dashboard Gino

A private dashboard to schedule, publish and measure the videos of an independent Italian creator.

Dashboard Gino is a self-hosted web application used by a single independent creator to manage his own animated-video accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. It runs only on the creator's own computer and connects only accounts he owns, through each platform's official sign-in. It is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • Publish and schedule. Upload a video once, write the caption for each platform and publish it now or at a chosen time.
  • Drafts. Send a video to the platform as a draft (for TikTok, to the creator's inbox) and finish it in the app.
  • Analytics. Follower counts, views and engagement of the creator's own accounts and posts, saved day by day.
  • Comments. Read and answer comments on the creator's own posts where the platform allows it.

Your data

Tokens, videos and statistics stay on the creator's own computer and are not sent to third parties. Access can be revoked at any time from the platform settings. Read the Privacy Policy, the Terms of Service and how to delete your data.
