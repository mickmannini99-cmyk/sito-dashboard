Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

This policy explains what Dashboard Gino (the "app") does with data from YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. The app is self-hosted: it runs only on the computer of the creator who uses it and connects only his own accounts. Contact: saucemeek@gmail.com.

Data we access

  • Account information: account or Page ID, name, username and profile picture.
  • Statistics: followers, views, likes, comments, shares and other insights of the creator's own accounts and posts.
  • Content: the list of posts and videos on the creator's own accounts, and the videos and captions uploaded through the app.
  • Comments on the creator's own posts, including the public name of who wrote them.
  • Access tokens issued by the platforms when an account is connected.

How we use it

  • To show the statistics of the creator's own accounts in the app.
  • To publish, schedule, check and delete the creator's own posts when he asks the app to.
  • To read and answer comments on the creator's own posts.

We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or training of any model, and we do not sell or share it with anyone.

Where it is stored

All data is stored only on the creator's own computer, in a local database that is not synchronised to any cloud service. Access tokens never leave that computer except when sent to the platform that issued them. Videos are sent directly to the platform they are published on; when Instagram requires a download link, the file is placed for a few minutes on the creator's own Facebook Page as an unpublished item or in his own private storage, and deleted as soon as Instagram has processed it.

Retention

  • Access tokens are deleted as soon as an account is disconnected in the app.
  • Statistics are kept as a history of the creator's own accounts until the account is removed from the app or deletion is requested.

Google user data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is accessed through the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Cookies

This website uses no cookies and no tracking. The app runs locally and uses no analytics.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, or deletion of, any data related to you by writing to saucemeek@gmail.com. See Data deletion. Users in the European Union may also lodge a complaint with their data protection authority.

Changes

Updates to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date.
