Data deletion

How to remove the data Dashboard Gino holds about you

Disconnect from the platform

  • Facebook and Instagram: Facebook Settings → Security and login → Business integrations → remove Dashboard Gino.
  • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions → remove Dashboard Gino.
  • TikTok: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions → remove Dashboard Gino.

Once access is removed, the app can no longer read or publish anything on that account.

Delete stored data

Write to saucemeek@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and the account name. We delete the access tokens and all statistics stored for that account within 30 days and confirm by email.
